BEHIND THE STORY

For the past six years in Thailand, a period of deep hardship, isolation, and silent suffering was endured with no space to speak and no one to listen with honesty, respect, and care.

Life has reached a profound turning point through the dignified care and support I have received in Australia. Even when faced with moments of disrespect toward one's humanity, the strength of the law has protected this journey, though the responsibility for that neglect remains.

Today, 27 July 2026, this journey from pain to purpose has truly given a new life and a renewed sense of hope, transforming silent endurance into a future built on safety and care.
